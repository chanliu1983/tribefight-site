Questions

How do I take a miracle?
Hold V (or LB on a controller) and the miracle wheel opens round the hand; point at one and let go. The hand now carries it — hold it over your land and click (or press A) to cast it. On iPhone or iPad, tap Miracles, tap a miracle, then tap the world to cast it. Miracles cost faith, and most can only be cast inside your own land.
How do I play on iPhone or iPad?
Tap to reach into the world. The on-screen Miracles and Build buttons open their wheels; tap an item to choose it. Drag with two fingers to turn or tilt the camera, and pinch to zoom in and out.
Why can't I claim that village?
Your land has to reach it — a chain of your relays, ring touching ring, back to one of your villages — and your fire there has to beat its owner's. The number over it shows your fire against what it takes. Light more campfires in villages joined to that chain, or send warriors into its ring.
My people stopped working.
Check the village's gates: a shut gate (red banner) stops everyone, your own people too, and work outside the wall waits. Click or tap the gate to open it. Otherwise, look at the stores: no food, and the village goes hungry.
A LAN game doesn't show up on the other device.
Both devices need the same version of the game (it is shown on the LAN screen) and the same network. On iPhone, iPad or Mac, allow Divine to find devices on the local network when asked; check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network (System Settings on Mac). Some guest or office Wi-Fi networks keep devices apart; in that case join by address — the host's lobby shows it.
Can I play with friends online?
On the App Store versions for iPhone, iPad and Mac, sign in to Game Center on the LAN screen and choose Play online. On Steam, choose "Host a game for Steam friends" and invite them from the lobby, or join theirs from your friends list.
Does it work with a controller / on the Steam Deck?
Yes. Every screen works with a controller; in play, the left stick moves the hand, the right stick moves the camera, and the shoulder buttons open the miracle and building wheels.
Where are my saves?
In the game's own data folder on your device — see the privacy policy for the list of files. Nothing is stored online.
