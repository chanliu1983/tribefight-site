# Divine — Support, Privacy & Marketing

A god game of tribes on a small round world: ten tribes, their fires and their warriors,
and ten civilisations' stories told as an old picture book.

**This repository is not the game.** It is the public web presence for Divine: the marketing
page, the privacy policy, and the place to report problems.

Three static files, English only. No cookies, no trackers, no external requests, no fonts
pulled from anywhere.

## Reporting a problem

**Open an issue:** <https://github.com/chanliu1983/tribefight-site/issues/new>

Say which version you play (Mac App Store, or Steam on Windows / macOS / Linux / Steam Deck),
what you were doing, and — for a campaign — which civilisation and chapter.
