Privacy Policy

Divine · © 2026 Tactic Master · last updated November 2026

Divine does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts to create, no email address to give, and nothing is sent to the developer — or anywhere else. The only network traffic is the multiplayer game you choose to play, with the players you choose to play with.

No accountsNo analyticsNo advertising No in-app purchasesNo crash reportingNo tracking

1. Summary

Divine runs on your own device. It has no sign-in of its own, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or telemetry library, and no crash or error reporting. It contains no in-app purchases. Single-player play, including the whole campaign, works with the network switched off.

2. What is stored on your device

The game writes a few files to its own data folder, in the place the operating system gives every app. They stay on your device and are never uploaded:

  • Settings — settings.cfg: volumes, controls, graphics and interface choices.
  • Saved games — the saves folder, and worlds.json: the worlds you keep.
  • Campaign progress — campaign_progress.json and the campaign folder: which chapters you have finished, and each chapter's last checkpoint.
  • Caches — planet.seed and a few plants_….bin files: the planet you last played and its pre-computed vegetation, so it loads faster.

Deleting the game removes them. There are no cookies and no identifier of any kind.

3. Network connections

The game opens a network connection only for multiplayer, and only when you ask for it:

  • LAN games. Hosting a game listens on your local network (port 7777), and the LAN screen announces and finds games with small broadcast messages on your local network (port 7780) — a game's name, its version, and how many players are in it. This traffic stays on your local network. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the system asks your permission first.
  • Game Center (App Store versions on iPhone, iPad and Mac). If you sign in to Game Center and play online, Apple's Game Center finds the other players and carries the game between you. That is Apple's service, under Apple's privacy policy; Divine receives only your Game Center display name, to show it to the other players in the match.
  • Steam (Steam version). If you run the game through Steam, it uses Steam for friends' lobbies, invitations, achievements and the status your friends see ("In a match"). That is Valve's service, under Valve's privacy policy; Divine receives only your Steam name and ID, to show you in your friends' games.

What goes between players in a match is the game itself: the world, the moves, and the player names. Nothing of it is kept after the match, and nothing is sent to the developer.

4. Third parties

No analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or telemetry SDK is included. The only third-party services are Apple Game Center (App Store versions on iPhone, iPad and Mac) and Steam (Steam version), and only when you use them for online play, as described above.

5. Children

Divine collects no personal data from anyone, children included. Online play goes through Game Center or Steam, whose own parental controls apply.
